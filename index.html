Learn about behavioural economics

The Default Effect

The Default Effect refers to the tendency for people to keep an option that has already been selected for them, even when they are free to change it.

Related concepts

Status Quo Bias

A preference for keeping things as they are rather than making a change.

Choice Architecture

The way choices are organised and presented can influence the decisions people make.

Nudging

A way of designing choices that gently influences behaviour while keeping other options available.

This summary is for learning purposes only. It is not a personality test and does not determine how easily you are influenced. Individual responses alone cannot establish a research finding.
