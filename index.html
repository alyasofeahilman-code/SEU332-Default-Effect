The Behavioral Economics behind your choices

Status Quo Bias

People tend to stick with the existing situation or option instead of making a change. A pre-selected option can become the option people leave unchanged.

Choice Architecture

The way choices are presented can influence decisions. For example, placing one option as the default may affect what people select.

Nudge Theory

A small change in how choices are presented can guide people towards an option without removing their freedom to choose differently.
