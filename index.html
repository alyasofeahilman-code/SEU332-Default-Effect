What is the Default Effect?

The Default Effect happens when people tend to keep an option that has already been set for them, rather than actively changing it. This may happen because keeping the option is convenient, saves time, or requires less effort.

Status Quo Bias

A tendency to prefer the current situation and avoid changing it.

Choice Architecture

The way choices are presented can influence how people decide.

Nudge

A small change in how options are presented that can guide behaviour without removing freedom of choice.
